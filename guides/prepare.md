# Before the audit: prepare a clean record

The goal is to pay the right premium. Start early, reconcile the records and preserve the paperwork that supports each proposed exclusion. Never alter a record to fit a desired outcome.

## 1. Get the full policy and the carrier’s instructions

Ask the carrier or your agent for the declarations, classification schedules, rating worksheets, coverage forms and **all endorsements**. A certificate of insurance is not the policy. Get the audit request, submission method, policy dates and deadline in writing. Ask which forms and date conventions the auditor expects.

Record the policy number, insured legal entities, locations, effective date, expiration or cancellation date, deposit premium and any midterm changes. Keep a copy of every submission and its receipt. Use a secure delivery method agreed with the recipient.

## 2. Collect the accounting package

| Record | Where / who to ask | What to check | Potential effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Policy-period P&L | Accountant or bookkeeper | Fiscal-year bridge, entities, locations | Explains payroll and revenue totals |
| Payroll register | Payroll provider | Employee, work dates, duties, class, gross pay, extra OT premium | Supports class splits and allowed deductions |
| General ledger detail | Accountant | Payroll, sales, subs, reimbursements and exclusion accounts | Finds duplicate or misallocated exposure |
| Four quarterly 941s | Payroll provider / accountant | Partial-quarter reconciliation to the policy | Explains differences from tax returns |
| 940 | Accountant | Annual payroll bridge | Checks completeness |
| Florida RT-6 reports | Payroll provider / Florida DOR account | Quarters intersecting policy dates | Corroborates state payroll |
| W-2s / W-3 | Payroll provider | Calendar year versus policy year | Explains wage totals |
| 1099s, sub ledger and contracts | Accountant / project manager | Entity, work description, dates and amounts | Separates subs from other vendors |
| Sub COIs | Sub or issuing agent | Correct entity, coverage line and all dates worked | May support WC charge reversal; GL depends on policy |
| Individual FL DWC exemptions | Officer and DWC | Eligibility, entity, industry and effective dates | May support eligible officer exclusion |

Taxable wages are not automatically premium payroll. Reconcile rather than forcing the reports to match. Keep the original exports plus a separate worksheet explaining every adjustment.

## 3. Build a WC exposure schedule

For each employee or supported group, list actual duties, class code from the policy, payroll for the exact period, extra overtime premium, any other proposed excluded pay and the supporting rule. Keep time records for class allocations; a job title or an unsupported percentage is not a payroll split.

For each officer, document the person’s role, period-specific applicable minimum and maximum, units (weekly / annual), weeks or other duration basis, and any eligible exemption. Do not apply one person’s cap once for every class. Put special situations such as leased employees, PEOs, maritime work or wrap-ups in the case notes and get specialist review.

Obtain the mod worksheet and each factor’s effective dates. If a factor changes during the policy, preserve actual exposure records for each segment; do not assume payroll is evenly distributed through the year.

## 4. Build a separate GL schedule

For every GL class, confirm the policy’s rating basis and denominator: payroll, gross sales, subcontract cost or units. Document exclusions individually. Separate insured and uninsured subcontractor costs, but do not remove insured costs merely because you have a COI. A different rate or retained charge may apply. See [GL](gl.md).

## 5. Resolve gaps and send a complete package

Use the app’s status values: missing, requested, received, verified or not applicable. For each item record who was asked, when, entity, dates covered, document reference and follow-up. “Received” is not “verified.” Explain non-applicable items.

Use the [carrier](templates/carrier-request.md), [accountant](templates/accountant-request.md) and [subcontractor](templates/subcontractor-request.md) drafts. Ask the auditor whether any records can follow later and confirm the deadline; do not assume an extension. Keep submitted totals and source documents together.

Sources and current-value checks: [Florida register](states/fl.md). F.S. 440.381 provides the Florida payroll audit context; the policy and applicable manuals determine the rating treatment.
