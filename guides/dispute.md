# Request a correction with evidence

A focused request is easier to review than a general objection to the bill. Identify the line, what the carrier used, the proposed correction, its authority and the record that supports it. Include increases as well as decreases when the reconciliation requires them.

## Build the packet

- **Cover letter:** insured, policy, period, audit date, contact, disputed items and requested action.
- **Findings:** one question per item; distinguish an established record fact from an interpretation to verify.
- **Evidence index:** document name, issuer, entity, relevant dates, page and the finding it supports. Only call an item “attached” when it is attached.
- **Proposed worksheet:** exposure, basis, denominator, rate, mod periods, premium sequence, final and deposit reconciliation. Mark unknowns TODO.
- **Requests:** full original worksheet, authority for rejected adjustments, confirmation of receipt, re-audit procedure and deadlines, and a corrected bill if supported.

Download the app’s Markdown draft or use [the packet template](templates/dispute-packet.md). It does not submit, sign or send the packet. Have a licensed professional review it before use.

## Route and deadline tracker

| Route | Use | Deadline to record | Authority to obtain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Written carrier dispute | Start here for WC and GL | **TODO: policy/carrier-specific** | Audit letter, policy and written carrier response |
| Carrier re-audit | Additional records or a corrected exposure schedule | **TODO: confirm acceptance window and requirements** | Carrier re-audit instructions and applicable manual / law |
| NCCI Dispute Resolution | Unresolved WC NCCI manual-rule matters, subject to state process | **TODO: confirm applicable filing limits and prerequisites** | Current Basic Manual state process / NCCI written confirmation |
| Florida DFS Consumer Services | Premium concerns and assistance with insurer response | **TODO: confirm any applicable limit** | DFS and policy-specific requirements |
| Florida OIR | Ask about the proper regulatory route | **TODO: confirm jurisdiction and timing** | OIR direction; obtain professional help for legal filing limits |

Do not substitute a generic number of days for the actual deadline. Keep the earliest confirmed date and its source in the case. Confirm what action preserves rights and whether a written extension is available. Asking for records, filing a complaint or requesting a re-audit does not automatically suspend payment, collection or other deadlines.

[NCCI’s overview](https://www.ncci.com/Articles/Pages/UW_disputeresolutionprocess.aspx) covers manual-rule disputes and prerequisites, including attempting resolution with the carrier and payment of undisputed premium. Confirm current state requirements directly; NCCI is not the GL route.

[Florida DFS insurance help](https://www.myfloridacfo.com/division/consumers/needourhelp) and [OIR contact information](https://www.floir.gov/about-us/contact-us) identify assistance routes. Do not use workers’ compensation medical reimbursement dispute procedures for an employer’s premium audit.

## After the response

Keep the carrier’s acceptance or rejection for every item. Reconcile a corrected bill to the accepted worksheet and deposit ledger. If a matter remains unresolved, organize the correspondence chronologically and ask the appropriate route about next steps. Do not stop communicating simply because you believe the bill is wrong.
