# Subcontractor insurance records request — DRAFT

To: [subcontractor / issuing agent]
Project / work: [description]
Legal entity paid: [name]
Dates worked: [start through end]

Please provide certificates showing the relevant workers’ compensation and general liability coverage for the entity and **every date worked**, including replacement policies where a renewal occurred. Please include issuer contact information so coverage and policy dates can be verified.

Please also confirm the work description, contracts and payment detail for these dates. If an individual Florida DWC exemption is relevant, provide the actual certificate, eligible individual, associated entity, applicable industry and effective dates for verification.

Please do not alter or backdate a document. If coverage did not apply to part of the period, identify that gap. Insurance documentation will be evaluated under the applicable policy; a COI does not automatically remove GL cost.

Please confirm a secure delivery method and reply by [requested date].

[name, role, date and contact]
