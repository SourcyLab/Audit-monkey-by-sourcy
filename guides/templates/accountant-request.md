# Accountant / payroll request — DRAFT

To: [accountant / bookkeeper / payroll provider]
For: [entity and policy period]
Needed by: [confirmed date]

Please help assemble a premium-audit reconciliation for the exact policy period:

- P&L and detailed general ledger, including sales and subcontractor accounts.
- Payroll registers by employee, actual duties, class and dates, with supported split records.
- Overtime detail separating straight-time and the extra premium portion, plus documentation of other proposed excluded pay.
- Four quarterly 941s, annual 940, Florida RT-6 reports, W-2s/W-3, 1099s and subcontractor payment detail.
- A calendar-year / tax-quarter bridge to the policy dates, identifying all adjustments and avoiding duplicate exposure.
- For GL, a reconciliation for each scheduled class basis (payroll, gross sales, sub cost or units), with evidence for each proposed exclusion.
- Officer payroll and duration information; policy/manual limits will be verified separately.

Please preserve original reports and put adjustments in a separate worksheet with source references. Please confirm a secure delivery channel before sending sensitive information.

[name, role, date and contact]
