# WC: payroll is the starting point, not the whole calculation

## Class allocation

Describe what people actually do. Compare their work with the policy’s classifications and applicable rules. Keep supported payroll splits with time and duty records. Do not assign a low-rate class because of a job title, and do not distribute payroll among classes using an unsupported guess. The engine accepts a verified class; it does not determine the correct code.

## Overtime and excluded pay

Gross payroll includes the ordinary pay for overtime hours. A proposed overtime deduction must identify **only the extra premium portion**, supported by payroll records and the applicable rule. Other excluded pay needs its own authority and records. Do not remove a full overtime wage line or assume all reimbursements, bonuses, vacation pay or severance are excluded.

## Officers and exemptions

Obtain the correct minimum and maximum for the person, policy-effective rules and time period. The app accepts **already-converted period dollar limits**. It does not contain Florida values, infer weekly limits or automatically prorate annual caps. Keep the conversion in notes.

Use employee-level rows for limits. If one officer works across classes or has a midterm mod / exemption change, allocate one applicable person-level limit under the governing rule before entering rows. Do not apply the full cap separately to each row. Obtain professional review for partial-period exemptions, PEOs, leased workers or other special situations.

Florida exemptions are individual and require eligibility and date checks. A certificate for a business name is not enough to assume every worker is exempt. Verify with [DWC](https://www.myfloridacfo.com/division/wc/employer/exemptions).

## Subcontractors

Reconcile the sub ledger to contracts, work performed and dates. A 1099 does not itself establish insured status or remove exposure. A WC COI must match the entity, coverage and dates worked; verify authenticity and applicable policy/manual treatment. The engine removes a sub row only when the reviewer explicitly verifies the conditions for exclusion. Use the correct chargeable exposure, not automatically all invoices or only labor, unless the applicable rule supports that choice.

## Mod and premium elements

Obtain the mod worksheet and effective dates. Each WC row must fall inside exactly one mod period. If a mod changes mid-policy, enter actual payroll for each segment. The engine refuses to guess a daily payroll allocation.

After row-level mods, match the policy’s sequence for credits, discounts, minimums, expense constant and assessments. A percentage assessment step uses the running subtotal; if the assessment uses a different base, enter a separately supported fixed charge. Actual audited payroll is never prorated again. Cancellation, short-rate penalties and special rating programs require separate verification and may need manual reconciliation.
